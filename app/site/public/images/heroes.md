# Hero Images

Published hero images for **The Velocity White Papers**. Every paper has an English and a French hero (the French is generated from the English, conditioned on it, with French labels), used for the site and print.

Each hero also has a **print-resolution upscale** (`hero-upscale.jpg`, at least 3000px high, upscaled via Claid.ai `digital_art`): landscape heroes are 4500×3000, portrait covers/cux4h-FR are 2000×3000.

Base URL: `https://thevelocitywhitepapers.com` — assets serve under `/public/images/`.
